How do habits work?

Habits work on a habit loop of a cue, routine, and reward, which the brain repeats to become an automatic behavior. The brain's basal ganglia and striatum store and automate these loops, shifting the behavior from conscious effort to subconscious action. This neurological process relies on repetition to strengthen the neural pathways, making the behavior more efficient and freeing up mental resources
